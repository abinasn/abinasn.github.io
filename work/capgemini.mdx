## What it was

My first job after college. I worked on the **SAP Enterprise Portal** for **Continental**, a global tyre manufacturer. Internal teams used the portal every day, including dashboards that showed **maintenance data**.

## What I did

I maintained and improved the portal's web pages:

- Fixed UI bugs and made pages look the same across browsers.
- Built small features and improved animations with **HTML**, **CSS** and **jQuery**.
- Connected pages to **REST APIs** to show live maintenance data on dashboards.

## What I learned

It was my first time working in a large codebase I didn't write. It taught me to read code carefully, make small safe changes, and work within a big team's process.

## Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery and SAP Enterprise Portal.
