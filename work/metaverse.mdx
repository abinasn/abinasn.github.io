## What it was

**MetaDemy** was a small ed-tech startup I co-founded. It offered affordable courses in **competitive coding** and **full-stack development** for students and working professionals who couldn't pay for expensive programs.

## What I did

As co-founder, I worked on both the product and the business:

- Built the platform with **PHP Laravel** and **MySQL**: course pages, student sign-up and a **payment gateway**.
- Hosted it on **AWS**, using **Redis** for caching.
- Helped with sales and with teaching the courses.

## Result

Our first batch had **23 students**, earned **₹2.7 lakh** in revenue, and **90%** of students completed the course. It taught me how to build a product and run a small business at the same time.

## Stack

PHP Laravel, MySQL, Redis and AWS.
